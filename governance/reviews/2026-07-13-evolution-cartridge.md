# Evolution cartridge candidate review — 2026-07-13

**Verdict:** ACCEPT WITH EDITS FOR CANDIDATE TESTING; HOLD FOR ADOPTION.

## Layer

Domain cartridge plus domain physical-continuity refinement and semantic fixtures.

## Fit

The proposal matches the general-system architecture when it refines shared identity, construct, causal, evidence, physical-continuity, and statistical contracts rather than creating a second kernel. It preserves exact observations while separating population products from processes and current causal roles from historical origin.

## Consistency

- Uses only canonical identity, construct, causal, and evidence-role vocabularies.
- Treats vocabulary as an inspection cue rather than a verdict trigger.
- Keeps selection, drift, mutation, migration, transmission, demography, and linkage as discriminable routes.
- Keeps current role, adaptation, etiological function, homology, conservation, optimality, and transport as separate target relations.
- Permits exact intervention or regime causality with partial internal mechanism while prohibiting mechanism language across open physical segments.

## Risks and calibration

- Historical-adaptation positive controls are now part of the required `evolution` semantic fixture set, but still require fresh live-provider execution to ensure the runtime neither rejects all historical inference nor launders coherent stories into causal closure.
- Formal, composite, and grouped fitness quantities have separate static and semantic identity controls. Fresh provider results must demonstrate that those generators remain separate.
- Drift and neutrality have a paired positive/negative semantic fixture requiring positive finite-sampling discrimination rather than failure to detect selection.
- Phylogenetic and replicate-lineage dependence have a semantic scope-separation fixture that preserves exact observations while denying nominal-row independence.
- Active-manifest integration on this branch is for candidate conformance. Merge remains blocked pending explicit adoption and fresh provider results.

## Pressure-test repairs

- Removed the runtime keyword-family list. Evolutionary vocabulary now appears only in adversarial fixtures and may trigger inspection, never a target-identity verdict.
- Removed the undeclared `unassigned` defaults. Selection, adaptation, and optimization receive only canonical relation-level dispositions after their targets are generated.
- Added constructed-measurement and grouping-handle branches for composite fitness, non-equivalent fitness quantities, and algorithmic optimality indices.
- Kept canonical identity-to-construct disposition mapping while adding a separate `construct_relation` expectation so resolved identity cannot erase construct dependence.
- Clarified why phylogenetic pseudoreplication can retain a measured cross-species segment while a one-lineage dataset cannot establish a replicated regime effect.
- Gave the two terminology guards explicit, non-overlapping scopes: conceptual or causal completion in the main cartridge and physical-segment completion in the physical cartridge.
- Added evolution vocabulary to the generic-fixture domain-leak guard.
- Replaced the near-global positive/negative fixture check with named causal-control assertions.
- Added `conformance/fixtures/evolution.json`, made it adoption-required, enabled `--fixture-set evolution`, and included every active cartridge and evolution physical-continuity contract in freshness hashing.

## Adoption-gate assessment

- **Goal and layer fit:** pass for candidate testing; the cartridge refines domain burdens without adding a causal currency.
- **Contradiction and redundancy:** no kernel vocabulary conflict detected; runtime lexical trigger architecture was removed.
- **Target identity and construct separation:** structurally covered, including conditional, constructed, grouping, unresolved, and rejected branches. `generated_identity_closed` remains available under the generic kernel but is not granted by domain default.
- **Physical continuity and metabolic binding:** structurally covered and included in the provider freshness hash.
- **Positive causal controls:** structurally present for manipulated selection, drift, historical adaptation, and direct molecular effects.
- **Semantic conformance:** harness complete; provider execution outstanding.
- **Provider result freshness:** no fresh evolution result bundles exist.
- **Adoption status:** hold. Structural success cannot substitute for the required independent provider runs with zero critical failures.

## Redundancy

The main cartridge owns relation reconstruction and domain burdens. The physical-continuity file owns carrier, transmission, population aggregation, and multi-generation chain templates. Generic dispositions and evidence roles remain in the shared kernel.

## Minimal safe form

Three cartridge files, one evolution semantic fixture set, conformance-runner and freshness integration, one dedicated structural validator, manifest entries under `domain_modules` only, and a candidate changelog fragment.

## Adoption status

Structural CI must remain green. Adoption additionally requires the configured independent provider runs for every critical generic, neuroscience, and evolution variant with zero critical failures. This review does not adopt the cartridge.
