## Fit

The electrochemical rule sharpens the existing requirements that signaling cannot act as a magic operator, causal admission is relation-specific, and exact intervention causality may coexist with a partial internal physical or metabolic route. It assigns gradients, membrane potentials, firing, and ionic propagation their default physical role without erasing a demonstrated pattern-to-transition contribution.

The ahistorical rule sharpens the existing recent-first discovery and source-authority rules. It makes architecture depend on admitted evidence and stable physical relations, not the status, date, chronology, or citation lineage of a theory or paper.

## Contradiction and consistency effects

- No conflict with the positive intervention control: controlled electrical-pattern variation may close the exact intervention-to-transition relation even when the downstream mechanism remains partial.
- No conflict with metabolic binding: substrate organization and metabolic support must be held constant or separately bounded for the pattern discriminator, while any unmeasured biological route remains explicit.
- No conflict with source boundaries: recent-first ordering applies only within the corpus the user permits.
- No conflict with older direct evidence: older observations, methods, datasets, disconfirmation, and physical-route evidence remain eligible when uniquely probative and are tested against the later record.
- No conflict with provenance: citation lineage may locate source trees and expose inherited assumptions, but cannot close identity, architecture, causality, bridges, or physical routes.

## Pressure-test findings, risks, and guards

### Electrochemical over-attribution

Risk: voltage, firing, or ionic flux could receive outcome-specific causal status from presence, magnitude, propagation, downstream covariation, or familiar neural language.

Guard: the default is motive-force or transmission. Outcome-specific causal organization requires controlled or otherwise discriminating variation in magnitude, polarity, waveform, timing, or spatial configuration that changes which transition occurs, while substrate organization, metabolic support, boundary conditions, and prior state are held or separately bounded.

### Electrochemical under-attribution

Risk: the default guard could erase a genuine partial causal contribution by patterned electrical variation.

Guard: the positive control requires preservation of the exact pattern-to-transition causal contribution when the intervention burden closes. It blocks only transfer to sole cause, untreated endogenous route, etiology, transport, or complete mechanism.

### Pattern manipulation with hidden co-manipulation

Risk: changing waveform or timing may also change tissue state, heating, substrate organization, metabolic support, or boundary conditions.

Guard: those variables must be held constant or separately bounded. A device setting or firing readout is not proof that the intended electrical pattern was the operative variable.

### Recent-first premature stopping

Risk: a hard recent-first order could become a date cutoff that excludes an older unique observation, method, dataset, disconfirmation, or physical-route record.

Guard: discovery continues backward for a specified uniquely probative contribution, then walks forward to test that contribution against replication, correction, null, and disconfirmation records.

### Foundational authority smuggling

Risk: canonical status or citation ancestry could re-enter as a substitute for target identity or explanatory architecture.

Guard: lineage has provenance and assumption-audit roles only. Every retained contribution passes ordinary admission, and architecture is generated only from admitted evidence and stable physical relations.

### “Stable physical relation” as a new authority label

Risk: a theory could be relabeled a stable physical relation and imported without reconstruction.

Guard: the phrase does not create a privileged source class. The relation must remain physically specified, recoverable, scope-bounded, and compatible with the current empirical and methodological record.
